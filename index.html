Client questions

Before you start.

Can MESHAQ build a house from the beginning?

Yes. Select the new-house construction service and provide your county, site status, plot information, desired rooms, style, budget and other requirements.

Can I upload a picture of the house I want?

Yes. The application allows you to upload reference images. For example, you can show the team a front elevation, roof style, floor-plan inspiration or another house you like.

Can I upload photos of my property if I want to sell?

Yes. Upload exterior, interior, compound, access-road and other useful property photographs.

Can I request land?

Yes. Provide the county, preferred location, approximate size, intended use and budget.

Are the house prices on the website quotations?

No. They are indicative ranges for planning and comparison. Actual construction costs depend on design, floor area, materials, finishes, site conditions, location and project scope.

What should I know before buying land?

Ownership, title, official searches, boundaries, restrictions, access, rates, planning considerations and other relevant matters should be independently verified before a purchase.

Can I talk to MESHAQ through WhatsApp?

Yes. The primary WhatsApp number is +254 712 242050.
