NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Not sure where to begin? Here are a few helpful starting points.

Ask about your project
Do you handle full house projects?

Full-house project enquiries are welcome. The exact scope, professionals involved, budget and timeline are confirmed after consultation and project review.

Can I request a quotation online?

Yes. Complete the project enquiry form and open the prepared WhatsApp message. A final quotation may require drawings, measurements, a site visit or other project information.

Do you work outside Kisumu?

You can submit an enquiry from any county in Kenya. Availability and logistics for each project will be discussed individually.

Can I share drawings or inspiration photos?

The demo form lets you select a file locally, but it does not upload it. You can send files directly in WhatsApp or use a secure upload feature after a backend is connected.

Are the gallery images completed company projects?

No. The current gallery is for design inspiration only. Verified company project photos and details should replace these references as they become available.

Are you NCA registered?

Please contact the company for current registration and licensing details. This website does not claim NCA registration without verified evidence.
