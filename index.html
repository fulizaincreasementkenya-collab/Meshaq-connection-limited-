Frequently asked questions

Questions clients usually have.

Can MESHAQ build my house from the beginning?

Yes. Select "Build a house from start to finish" in the application and provide your county, location, land status, requirements and budget.

Can I find land through MESHAQ?

Yes. Submit your preferred county, location, size, intended use and budget. Availability and ownership must be verified before any purchase.

Can I sell my land or house?

Yes. Submit a property-for-sale application containing the location, size, asking price and available ownership/document information.

Are the house prices fixed?

No. The prices shown are indicative ranges. A proper quotation depends on the design, size, materials, finishes, site, location and scope.

How does the WhatsApp application work?

Complete the application and press "SEND APPLICATION TO MESHAQ". Your phone opens WhatsApp with a prepared message containing your information.
