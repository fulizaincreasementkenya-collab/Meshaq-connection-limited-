TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Engineering & Technical Works, Handled with Care

We help clients plan and coordinate the technical parts of a build, matching each project with the right qualified expertise where required.

01

Structural & civil works

Foundations, reinforced concrete, masonry, drainage and civil works coordination.

02

Architectural planning

Space planning, drawings, design coordination and support for relevant approvals.

03

Electrical systems

Lighting, wiring, power points and electrical installation by qualified personnel.

04

Plumbing & sanitation

Water supply, waste-water drainage, sanitary ware and plumbing upgrades.

05

Costing & materials

Cost estimates, material schedules and practical budget discussions.

06

Site supervision

Work sequencing, quality checks within agreed scope and progress communication.

Professional standards matter.

Where a task requires a licensed engineer, architect, quantity surveyor, electrician or other regulated specialist, the work should be assessed, designed, approved or carried out by an appropriately qualified professional in line with Kenyan requirements.

Professional standards & compliance: Technical designs, statutory approvals, inspections and all regulated work are undertaken, reviewed or signed off by appropriately qualified and registered professionals in Kenya, as required by law. Project-specific responsibilities and approvals are confirmed before work begins.
